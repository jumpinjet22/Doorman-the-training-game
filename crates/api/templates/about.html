About this game

Every day you get a handful of lines someone said to a smart doorbell, and you guess why they're at the door, or pick the better of two replies the doorbell could give.

What your answers are for

None of these visitors are real. A computer program wrote every line, and a person checked it before it reached you. Some of what it writes is off: the wrong reason for the visit, or a reply nobody would want to hear at their door. Your answers, together with everyone else's, decide which lines are good enough to learn from and which get thrown out.

Your answers train a model. Once the crowd agrees on a card, that answer becomes training data for a doorbell assistant: what visitors want, and which replies are better. Only the crowd's settled answer is used, never one player's answer on its own.

Some questions have an answer we already know. They help us tell careful players from random clicking, so careful answers count for more. We never say which ones they are.

What we keep about you

  • A random ID in a cookie, so we know which answers are yours. No name, no email, no account.
  • Your answers, how long each one took, and how sure you said you were.
  • A leaderboard name, only if you choose one. Anyone can see it.
  • We never store your IP address. To stop floods of fake players, we keep a scrambled code for your network for a day.
  • Cloudflare checks each vote comes from a person.
