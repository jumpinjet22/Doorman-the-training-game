About this game

Every day you get a handful of lines someone said to a smart doorbell, and you guess why they're at the door, or pick the better of two replies the doorbell could give.

What your answers are for

None of these visitors are real. A computer program wrote every line to help train KNOCK, a doorbell assistant. Some of what it writes is off: the wrong reason for the visit, or a reply nobody would want to hear at their door. Your answers, together with everyone else's, decide which lines are good enough to learn from and which get thrown out.

Some questions have an answer we already know. They help us tell careful players from random clicking, so careful answers count for more. We never say which ones they are.

What we keep about you

  • A random ID in a cookie, so we know which answers are yours. No name, no email, no account.
  • Your answers, and how long each one took.
  • We never store your IP address. To stop floods of fake players, we keep a scrambled code for your network for a day.
  • Cloudflare checks each vote comes from a person.
